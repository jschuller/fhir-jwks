Personal Health Record Sync

Terms of use and privacy statement. Last updated 2026-09-17.

What this app is

A personal, read-only tool that copies one patient's own health records from a healthcare organization's Epic patient-access FHIR API into a database on that patient's own computer. It is operated by its author for the author's own records. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its author.

Data handling

  • Data is read only after the patient logs in through MyChart and approves access.
  • Data is stored only on a computer the patient controls, on an encrypted disk. It is never transmitted to the app author's servers, to third parties, or to any cloud service.
  • The app never modifies, creates, or deletes anything in the health record.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from MyChart under linked apps, which invalidates the app's tokens at that organization.

Security

The app authenticates with a private key held only on the patient's computer. The matching public keys are published at jwks.json (production) and sandbox/jwks.json.
